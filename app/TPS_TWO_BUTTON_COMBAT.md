# TPS Two-Button Combat

TPS LOCK-ON BATTLE uses the virtual 8-way lever plus two touch buttons:

- **ATTACK**: automatically selects punch-range attacks up close and kick-range attacks farther out. Repeated taps buffer a three-hit combo.
- **STEP**: bursts in the held lever direction. Side steps evade strikes and open a short flank-punish window; back steps evade or create space.
- **Forward STEP + ATTACK**: cancels the forward step into the dash attack.

The classic DUEL mode keeps its original P / K / G control layout.
